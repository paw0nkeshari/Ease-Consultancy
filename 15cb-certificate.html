15CB Certificate

Accountant certification support for qualifying taxable foreign-remittance transactions, including review of the remittance nature, taxability and applicable withholding provisions.

What this service can cover

  • Remitter and beneficiary information review
  • Remittance amount and bank-detail compilation
  • Nature and purpose of remittance review
  • Taxability and TDS-rate analysis by the CA
  • DTAA information review where applicable
Genuine context:
The Income Tax Department currently describes Form 15CB as an accountant's certificate for specified taxable payments to non-residents / foreign companies. Its current portal documentation also maps the corresponding form under the Income Tax Act, 2025 to Form 146.

We help organise the relevant information and supporting documents so the certificate can be prepared for review by the authorised professional or receiving authority.
