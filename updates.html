Business & Compliance Updates

Selected updates available as of 09 November 2026. Always open the notification and verify applicability, effective dates and any later amendments before acting.

RBI: Cyber resilience for non-bank payment operators

05 November 2026 · RBI

RBI published Master Directions on Cyber Resilience and Digital Payment Security Controls for non-bank Payment System Operators. Relevant entities should review applicability, governance, security controls, incident response and implementation timelines in the direction.
Open RBI notifications

FEMA: Export and import regulations amended

03 November 2026 · RBI

The Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026 were notified. Businesses engaged in cross-border trade should check the amended regulation and effective-date provisions with their authorised dealer bank or adviser.
Open RBI FEMA notification register

RBI: Foreign liabilities and assets census

06 November 2026 · RBI

RBI released provisional results of the 2025–26 Census on Foreign Liabilities and Assets of Indian Direct Investment Entities. Indian entities with foreign direct investment or overseas direct investment should review the applicable annual reporting requirements and deadlines.
Check RBI publications

GST: Central Tax (Rate) notifications

28 October 2026 · CBIC

CBIC's tax information portal lists Central Tax (Rate) notifications dated 28 October 2026, including a special procedure under section 148 relating to the Toll-Operate-Transfer model and an amendment to Notification 17/2017-Central Tax (Rate). Review the exact notification text before applying any rate or procedure.
Open CBIC Tax Information Portal

GSTN: Registration and portal process advisories

01 November 2026 · GSTN

Recent GSTN advisories cover GST registration amendment workflows, composition levy functionality, cancellation approval, simplified registration under Rule 14B and changes to FORM GST REG-01. Taxpayers should check the GST portal advisory itself before submitting or amending registration details.
Open GST portal

Income Tax: CBDT notification register

November 2026 · CBDT

The Income Tax Department's official notification register is the primary place to verify recent CBDT notifications, corrigenda and rule changes, including November 2026 publications. Confirm the notification number, statutory provision and commencement date before relying on a summary.
Open CBDT notifications
