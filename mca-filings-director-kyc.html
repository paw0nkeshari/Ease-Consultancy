Service Details

Keep corporate records current and organized

Corporate compliance involves maintaining accurate company information and submitting applicable statutory forms within the prescribed framework. We can help prepare checklists, coordinate documents and organize filing records.

What we can assist with

  • Annual filing support
  • Financial statement / annual return coordination
  • Director appointment or change filing support
  • DIN / Director KYC assistance
  • Filing status and document tracking
Genuine information:
MCA records include filings such as financial statements and annual returns. Importantly, from 31 March 2026, the annual director KYC requirement was replaced by a simpler KYC intimation once every three years, subject to the applicable rules and circumstances.
