Startup / Share Valuation

Valuation support for startups, equity shares and transaction-related requirements, with analysis tailored to the company's stage, capital structure, purpose and available information.

Designed around the purpose of the valuation.

We organise the assignment around the intended use, available evidence, applicable requirements and the level of analysis needed for a clear, defensible report.

Typical Use Cases

  • Equity issuance or transfer discussions
  • Investor and fundraising documentation
  • Shareholder or transaction planning
  • Tax or regulatory valuation requirements where applicable

Service Scope

  • Review the company's incorporation, ownership and capital structure information.
  • Analyse historical performance, business model, market opportunity and operating assumptions.
  • Consider the company's funding stage, dilution, preference rights and other relevant capital terms.
  • Apply an appropriate valuation framework considering the purpose and available evidence.
  • Document assumptions, limitations and the resulting valuation analysis.

Information Usually Required

  • Certificate of incorporation and constitutional documents, where relevant
  • Capitalisation table / shareholding pattern
  • Financial statements and management accounts
  • Details of funding rounds and securities issued
  • Business plan and projections
  • Material agreements relevant to valuation, if applicable

Expected Deliverables

  • Share/equity valuation analysis
  • Methodology and assumptions
  • Relevant sensitivity or scenario analysis, where appropriate
  • Purpose-specific valuation report / working paper
Important: Startup valuations can be highly sensitive to projections, financing terms and market conditions. The final approach should be aligned with the specific legal, tax or transaction purpose.
