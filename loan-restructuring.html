Service Details

Loan Restructuring

Advisory support for borrowers facing genuine repayment stress and exploring a viable resolution with their lender.

What this service can cover

  • Cash-flow and repayment-stress assessment
  • Current debt and obligation mapping
  • Viability and turnaround information preparation
  • Resolution / restructuring proposal documentation
  • Lender discussion and follow-up support
Genuine business context:
RBI guidance states that restructuring decisions should be based on financial viability and reasonable certainty of repayment; assessment can include cash flows and viability benchmarks such as debt-service coverage and return measures.

Stress Recovery

We help organize the information and documentation required for a clearer professional discussion with the relevant lender or agency.
