# Ease Consultancy Website

Ease Consultancy is a professional service website for accounting, taxation, audit, compliance, financial and business advisory services.

## About the Website

The website is designed to present professional services in a clear, modern and user-friendly format. It provides service information for individuals, businesses, entrepreneurs and organisations and helps visitors understand available professional support.

## Main Service Areas

- Income Tax Services
- GST & Tax Compliance
- Audit & Assurance Services
- Concurrent Audit
- Forensic Audit
- Due Diligence
- Stock Audit
- Process Audit
- Financial & Business Advisory
- Other Accounting and Compliance Services

## Key Website Features

- Professional and responsive UI
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Service-based navigation
- Dedicated sub-pages for individual services
- Detailed service descriptions
- Business and individual applicability information
- Practical benefits and risk/consequence information
- Official-source based regulatory information where applicable
- Contact / enquiry call-to-action sections
- Internal navigation between related services
- Clean typography and professional colour scheme
- Font Awesome icons
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile devices

## Content Approach

Service pages are designed to explain:

1. What the service is
2. What the service covers
3. How the service is useful
4. Who may benefit from it
5. Applicability to businesses
6. Applicability to individuals
7. Who may not normally need it
8. Possible circumstances or risks if important compliance or review requirements are ignored
9. Relevant official/regulatory information
10. Professional limitations and disclaimers

Where regulatory information is included, the website should use current official sources such as the Income Tax Department, CBDT, RBI, SEBI, MCA or ICAI, depending on the subject.

## Important Disclaimer

The information presented on the website is intended for general information and service-awareness purposes. Regulatory requirements, tax provisions, audit requirements and compliance obligations can change.

Visitors should verify the applicable law, rules, regulations, notifications and professional requirements for their specific circumstances. Professional advice should be obtained where required.

## Deployment

- Other compatible static web hosting services

For a custom domain, configure the domain's DNS records according to the hosting provider's instructions.

## Maintenance

Before publishing updates:

1. Keep a backup of the current website.
2. Preserve existing filenames and internal links.
3. Test every navigation link.
4. Test all service sub-pages.
5. Check the website on desktop and mobile.
6. Verify contact information.
7. Review regulatory information against official sources.
8. Check external links and official references.
9. Test the website after deployment.
