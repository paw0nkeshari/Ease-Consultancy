Intangible Asset Valuation

Valuation support for identifiable and other intangible assets such as brands, goodwill, intellectual property and commercially valuable rights.

Designed around the purpose of the valuation.

We organise the assignment around the intended use, available evidence, applicable requirements and the level of analysis needed for a clear, defensible report.

Typical Use Cases

  • Business acquisition and transaction analysis
  • Strategic planning and portfolio decisions
  • Licensing, transfer or commercialisation discussions
  • Accounting, tax or regulatory purposes where applicable

Service Scope

  • Identify the intangible asset, ownership rights, useful context and valuation purpose.
  • Review available legal, commercial and financial evidence supporting the asset.
  • Assess relevant economic benefits, useful life, market position and key risks.
  • Select an appropriate valuation approach, which may include income, market or cost-based analysis depending on the asset and purpose.
  • Document assumptions, limitations and the resulting valuation conclusion.

Information Usually Required

  • Trademark, copyright, patent or other IP details, where applicable
  • Ownership and licensing agreements
  • Brand or product information
  • Revenue or cash-flow information attributable to the asset, where available
  • Market and competitor information
  • Relevant acquisition or transaction documents

Expected Deliverables

  • Intangible asset valuation analysis
  • Methodology and key assumptions
  • Supporting calculations / sensitivity analysis where appropriate
  • Purpose-specific valuation report
Important: Intangible assets can require specialised assumptions and evidence. The valuation method should reflect the nature of the asset, available data and the intended use of the report.
