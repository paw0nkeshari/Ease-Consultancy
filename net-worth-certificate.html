Net Worth Certificate

Professional financial-position documentation prepared from the applicant's supporting financial records for a stated purpose such as a visa application, lending assessment or other legitimate requirement.

What this service can cover

  • Review of assets and liabilities supported by records
  • Compilation of the net-worth statement
  • Cross-checking ownership / investment evidence where applicable
  • Purpose-specific certificate wording and presentation
  • Supporting-document checklist for the issuing professional
Genuine context:
Net worth is generally determined by comparing the value of assets with liabilities. The certificate should be based on records and evidence appropriate to the purpose for which it is being issued.
